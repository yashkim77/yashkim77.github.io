Why hire me

A few reasons I’m effective in product roles that require depth, judgment, and execution.

01

I enjoy turning messy, complex problems into clear product solutions.

One of the biggest products I led at Labra was our co-sell automation platform, connecting Salesforce and HubSpot with AWS Partner Central.

Before this, customers managed the same opportunity across multiple systems — creating records manually, updating them separately, and dealing with duplicate data and inconsistent information throughout the sales cycle.

Instead of improving one step at a time, we redesigned the workflow so CRM and AWS Partner Central stayed synchronized automatically. That removed repetitive work for sales teams, improved data quality, and shortened the overall deal lifecycle by around ~65%.

02

I tie product decisions to measurable outcomes.

One challenge I saw was that sales teams often worked opportunities based on intuition instead of identifying which accounts were most likely to convert.

To solve this, I led the development of a propensity-based account scoring model that combined firmographic, technographic, and engagement signals into a single prioritization framework. Rather than just giving teams more data, we helped them focus on the right accounts.

The result was a 40% increase in qualified deal volume, giving sales teams a clearer way to prioritize their pipeline.

03

My engineering background helps me build with stronger judgment.

Many product decisions break down because engineering and product aren't solving the same problem or estimating work from different perspectives.

Before moving into Product Management, I spent three years building Labra's integration platform and APIs as a Software Engineer. That experience helps me work closely with engineers, understand technical trade-offs, and scope solutions that balance customer value with engineering effort. The impact is better collaboration, faster decision-making, and product plans that are grounded in technical reality instead of assumptions.

04

I use systems thinking and tools to scale execution.

Across the product lifecycle—from customer research and documentation to product operations and execution—I look for repetitive work that can be automated without sacrificing quality.

At Labra, I built AI-powered workflows to summarize customer and sales calls, extract product insights, generate meeting notes and action items, assist with PRD drafting, monitor competitors, create recurring product reports, and streamline knowledge sharing and onboarding.

To me, AI isn't just another tool — it's a way to eliminate repetitive work and help teams move faster. Whether it's research, documentation, analysis, or internal workflows, I use AI wherever it creates meaningful leverage across the product lifecycle.
